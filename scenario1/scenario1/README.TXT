PROJECT TITLE: Dungeon (card-combat prototype)

PURPOSE OF PROJECT:
Dungeon crawler where fights are played as a trading card game.
Work in progress - the card battle system is about half done.

HOW TO START THIS PROJECT:
Press Run. (To test a fight on its own: right-click BattleWorld > new BattleWorld().)

USER INSTRUCTIONS:
Dungeon: arrow keys to move. Touch an enemy to start a battle.
Battle:  you get 3 energy and 5 cards each turn.
         Click a card to play it. Single-target attacks then need a click on an enemy.
         Click End Turn when done. Enemies show what they will do above their heads.
         Win = new card + a little healing. Lose = click to restart.

DONE:    3 enemy types, multi-enemy fights, 8 cards, draw/discard/reshuffle,
         block, healing, card draw, card reward, health carried between fights.
TODO:    status effects (poison, weak), more enemies and bosses, shop/rest rooms,
         deck viewer, multiple dungeon rooms, sounds and animations, balance pass.
